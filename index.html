How We Work

From your first idea to a live website, we keep the process simple and transparent. We understand what you need, guide you through decisions, and build your website step by step.

01.

Understand Your Goal

First, we understand why you need a website and what you want it to achieve. Based on your business and goals, we'll share our suggestions and help you decide the right direction.

02.

Understand Your Requirements

You don't need to be a website expert. Tell us what you have in mind — even if you're not sure what you need. We'll explain the options and help you figure it out.

03.

Design Your Website

Once we understand your requirements, we create the visual design and overall look. We'll make sure the design matches your business and customer experience goals.

04.

Prepare Your Content

After the design is clear, we'll work on the content your website needs — text, images, services, business information — and organize everything properly.

05.

Bring Design to Life

Once design and content are ready, we move into development and build the responsive front-end so your website works smoothly across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

06.

Add Smart Functionality

We can add useful features using Google Apps Script — such as contact form submissions, email notifications, and sending enquiry data directly to Google Sheets.

07.

Launch Your Website

Once everything is tested and you're happy with the result, we'll help get your website live with the appropriate hosting and deployment setup based on your requirements.
